
☕ BRIGHT COFFEE SHOP SALES ANALYSIS

# 1. Project Overview

The Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis is a data analytics project focused on analysing sales performance over six months, from January to June 2023.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that help the business understand sales trends, product performance, store performance and customer purchasing patterns.

The project aims to support data-driven decision-making by identifying opportunities to improve sales, manage inventory and increase business performance.

# 2. Methodology

The project followed a structured data analytics process:

# Data Collection
- Worked with approximately 61,078 sales records covering January to June 2023.
- The dataset included transaction dates, quantities, store locations, product details, categories and unit prices.

# Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Checked for missing and inconsistent values.
- Identified and removed duplicate records where necessary.
- Corrected formatting issues in the unit price column.
- Ensured that dates, quantities and prices were correctly formatted.
- Prepared the dataset for analysis.

# Data Analysis
Analysed the data to answer key business questions, including:
- What was the total revenue generated?
- Which products sold the most units?
- Which categories generated the highest sales?
- How did the stores perform?
- Which months recorded the highest revenue?
- What time of day generated the most revenue?
- How consistent were sales throughout the week?

Revenue was calculated using:

**Revenue = Quantity Sold × Unit Price**

# Data Visualisation
Created dashboards to present the findings through:
- Overall sales performance.
- Monthly revenue trends.
- Product and category performance.
- Store performance.
- Sales by time of day.

# 3. Summary of Key Findings

-Store Performance
Sales performance across the stores was relatively balanced, with no major differences in their contributions.

-Product Category Performance
Coffee and tea were the leading product categories, contributing significantly to overall sales.

-Product Performance
A small number of products accounted for a large proportion of units sold, showing that certain products were more popular than others.

-Monthly Sales Trends
Sales showed improvement after February, indicating positive growth during the following months.

-Sales by Time of Day
Morning hours generated the highest revenue, making this the strongest sales period.

-Weekly Sales Performance
Weekly revenue remained relatively consistent, indicating stable sales performance throughout the week.

# 4. Recommendations

-Focus on Best-Selling Products
Maintain sufficient stock of popular coffee and tea products to meet customer demand and avoid stock shortages.

-Improve Product Marketing
Introduce promotions and marketing strategies for products with lower sales to improve their performance.

-Strengthen Morning Operations
Ensure sufficient staffing, stock availability and efficient customer service during peak morning hours.

-Explore Evening Sales Opportunities
Introduce evening promotions, product combinations and targeted marketing to encourage more purchases.

-Maintain Consistent Store Performance
Monitor individual store performance to identify opportunities for improvement and maintain consistency across locations.

-Monitor Monthly Sales Trends
Regularly track monthly revenue to identify growth opportunities, seasonal patterns and changes in customer purchasing behaviour.

# 5. Project Conclusion

The Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis demonstrates how data analytics can help businesses understand sales performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

Through data cleaning, SQL analysis and dashboard visualisation, raw sales data was transformed into meaningful business insights.

The project highlights the importance of data-driven decision-making in improving product performance, managing inventory, understanding sales trends and supporting business growth.
